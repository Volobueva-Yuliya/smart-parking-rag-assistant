# Central Plaza Parking Knowledge Base
## General Information

Central Plaza Parking is an underground parking facility in Tbilisi, Georgia.
This parking facility provides 120 total parking spaces and is designed for short-term and mid-term parking.

The parking includes:
- Standard parking spaces for regular vehicles
- Electric vehicle (EV) charging spaces
- Accessible parking spaces for people with disabilities

The parking operates with automated entry and exit barriers, security cameras, and digital payment systems.
This parking is suitable for passenger cars and small SUVs. Large commercial vehicles are not allowed.
Users can park without a reservation or reserve a parking space in advance using the chatbot system.

## Working Hours

The parking working hours are from 07:00 to 23:00 every day.

Entry rules:
- Vehicles can enter the parking from 07:00 until 22:30

Exit rules:
- Vehicles can exit the parking until 23:30

Night parking policy:
- Overnight parking is NOT allowed
- Vehicles must leave the parking before closing time (23:00)

If a vehicle remains after closing time:
- Additional service fees may apply
- Staff assistance may be required for exit

## Pricing

Parking price:
- Standard parking cost is 3.5 GEL per hour

Free parking:
- The first 15 minutes are free

EV charging price:
- Additional 1 GEL per hour for electric vehicle charging

Lost ticket fee:
- Fixed fee of 20 GEL

Payment methods accepted:
- Cash
- Bank card
- Mobile application

Charges are calculated based on total parking duration and rounded to the nearest hour.

## Parking Rules

General parking rules:
- Maximum speed inside parking: 10 km/h
- Follow all road signs and markings
- Park only in designated parking spaces
- Keep your parking ticket until exit

Vehicle restrictions:
- Maximum vehicle height: 2.1 meters
- Only passenger vehicles are allowed
- Trailers and oversized vehicles are not allowed

Behavior rules:
- Engine idling is limited to 2 minutes
- Dangerous driving is prohibited
- Blocking access lanes is not allowed

Safety:
- Parking is monitored by cameras
- Users are responsible for their vehicles and belongings

## Space Types

Parking space types available:

Standard parking spaces:
- For regular passenger vehicles
- No special restrictions

Electric vehicle (EV) parking spaces:
- Equipped with charging stations
- Intended for electric vehicles
- Additional charging fee applies

Accessible parking spaces (for people with disabilities):
- Reserved for drivers with disabilities
- Located close to entrances
- Require a valid accessibility permit

Important:
- Accessible parking spaces are ONLY for people with disabilities
- EV spaces should be used only for charging
- Misuse of special parking spaces may result in penalties

## Booking Policy

Users can reserve a parking space using the chatbot.

Required booking information:
- First name
- Last name
- Vehicle plate number
- Reservation start time
- Reservation end time

Booking process:
1. The chatbot collects booking information step by step
2. The system checks parking availability
3. The reservation is submitted for manual confirmation (human-in-the-loop)

Important:
- A reservation is valid only after confirmation
- A pending reservation does not guarantee a parking space

## Cancellation Policy

Users can cancel a parking reservation using the chatbot.

Free cancellation:
- Allowed up to 2 hours before reservation start time

Late cancellation:
- Less than 2 hours before start → 50% charge applies

No-show policy:
- If the user does not arrive, the reservation is non-refundable

Refunds:
- Processed automatically after cancellation
